About

Lviv, Ukraine

Hi, I’m Andrii — a product designer who likes to build things.

I’m a product designer based in Lviv, Ukraine. I design end-to-end flows and high-fidelity interfaces in Figma, from requirements and use cases all the way to developer handoff.

I started with web design in 2016, moved through UI and UX roles, and today work on fintech products such as a loan management platform. I enjoy discovery sessions, research, journey maps and turning business and user needs into clear information architecture.

My background is in accounting and finance, which is why I care about the business side of design: goals, trade-offs and numbers. Lately I’m exploring Figma-to-code workflows and AI-assisted tooling in design-system work.
