Approach

Design that moves the numbers

I start with people, measure with metrics and use AI to move faster — without handing over the judgment. Here is how I work from first question to shipped result.

Principles

Four things I optimise for

UX first

Good products start with a clear picture of the person and the job they are trying to get done.

  • Qualitative and quantitative research before pixels
  • User flows, scenarios and information architecture before UI polish
  • Journey maps, JTBD, storyboards and empathy maps when they help
  • Accessibility (WCAG) built in, not bolted on

Business impact

Design is a lever for the business: revenue, cost, risk and speed. With a degree in accounting and finance, I speak the language of the business.

  • Turn business needs into user flows and requirements
  • Prioritise by impact, effort and confidence
  • Align with PMs, developers and stakeholders in discovery sessions
  • Ship small, learn fast, scale what works

Product metrics

If we cannot say how we will know it worked, we are guessing. Every project gets a metric and a hypothesis.

  • Activation, conversion, retention and time-to-value
  • Funnels and cohorts to find the real drop-off
  • A/B tests and staged rollouts where it makes sense
  • Quantitative data paired with qualitative insight

AI as a collaborator

AI speeds up the work I already know how to do well. It does not replace taste, empathy or accountability.

  • Synthesising research notes and feedback
  • Exploring Figma-to-code workflows with AI-assisted tooling
  • Rapid prototypes, copy variations and edge-case checks
  • Human review for everything that reaches users

Process

From question to measurable result

The loop repeats: what I measure feeds the next round of discovery.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Talk to users, read the data, understand the business goal.

  2. 02

    Define

    Frame the problem, pick the metric, agree on success.

  3. 03

    Design

    Explore options fast with sketches, prototypes and AI.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Ship with engineering, in small and testable slices.

  5. 05

    Measure

    Check the numbers, learn, iterate or move on.

Metrics

From goal to number

A simple chain I use to connect design decisions to business results. The example numbers are placeholders.

  1. Goal Increase trial-to-paid conversion
  2. Metric Activation rate in the first 7 days
  3. Hypothesis A guided checklist reduces time-to-value
  4. Experiment A/B test, 2 weeks, 50/50 split
  5. Result Placeholder: +X% activation, −Y% time-to-value
ActivationDo new users reach the “aha” moment?
ConversionDo they sign up, upgrade, buy?
RetentionDo they come back and keep using it?
Time-to-valueHow quickly do they get the benefit?
Task successCan people finish what they came to do?
CSAT / NPSHow do they feel, in their own words?

Working with AI

AI is my fastest junior teammate.

I use AI to get to a first draft sooner, so there is more time for the human parts: choosing what to build and why. I completed Anthropic’s “AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations” course and apply it in my day-to-day design-system work.

Where it helps

  • Summarising interviews and feedback
  • Generating options and prototypes
  • Writing and testing UI copy
  • Spotting gaps, edge cases, a11y issues

My guardrails

  • Humans decide; AI proposes
  • No private user data in public tools
  • Always verify facts and outputs
  • Test with real users before shipping
